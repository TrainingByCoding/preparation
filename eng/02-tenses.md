# All 12 English Tenses — Master Guide

> English has **3 time zones** (Past / Present / Future) × **4 aspects** (Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous) = **12 tenses**

---

## The Big Map

```
                SIMPLE          CONTINUOUS        PERFECT         PERFECT CONTINUOUS
PRESENT     I work            I am working      I have worked    I have been working
PAST        I worked          I was working     I had worked     I had been working
FUTURE      I will work       I will be working I will have      I will have been
                                                worked           working
```

---

## PRESENT TENSES

### 1. Present Simple
**Formula:** `Subject + V1 (base verb)` | `He/She/It + V1 + s/es`

**Use for:**
- Habits / routines: _I wake up at 7am._
- Facts / truths: _The sun rises in the east._
- Schedules: _The train leaves at 9._

```
(+) I work every day.
(-) I don't work every day.
(?) Do you work every day?

(+) She works hard.
(-) She doesn't work hard.
(?) Does she work hard?
```

**Signal words:** always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week

---

### 2. Present Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + am/is/are + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Happening RIGHT NOW: _I am eating right now._
- Temporary situations: _She is staying at a hotel._
- Near future plans: _We are meeting tomorrow._
- Annoying habits (with "always"): _He is always interrupting me!_

```
(+) I am studying English.
(-) I am not studying English.
(?) Are you studying English?

(+) She is working.
(-) She is not working.
(?) Is she working?
```

**Signal words:** now, right now, at the moment, currently, these days, look!, listen!

> ⚠️ Verbs NOT used in continuous:
> `know, believe, understand, love, hate, want, need, own, seem`
> ❌ I am knowing the answer. → ✅ I know the answer.

---

### 3. Present Perfect
**Formula:** `Subject + have/has + V3 (past participle)`

**Use for:**
- Life experience (ever/never): _I have been to Japan._
- Recent action with present result: _She has lost her keys._ (still lost now)
- Unfinished time periods: _I haven't eaten today._
- News/announcements: _Scientists have discovered a new planet._

```
(+) I have finished my work.
(-) I haven't finished my work.
(?) Have you finished your work?

(+) He has lived here for 5 years.
(-) He hasn't lived here long.
(?) Has he lived here long?
```

**Signal words:** just, already, yet, ever, never, recently, since, for, so far

---

### 4. Present Perfect Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + have/has + been + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Activity that started in the past and is STILL happening: _I have been studying for 3 hours._
- Recent activity with visible result: _She looks tired — she has been working all night._

```
(+) I have been waiting for an hour.
(-) I haven't been waiting long.
(?) How long have you been waiting?

(+) He has been running.          ← (explains why he's sweating now)
```

**Signal words:** for, since, all day, all morning, how long, lately

---

## PAST TENSES

### 5. Past Simple
**Formula:** `Subject + V2 (past form)`

**Use for:**
- Completed action at a specific past time: _I called him yesterday._
- Series of completed events: _She woke up, had breakfast, and left._
- Past habits (with "used to"): _I used to play football._

```
(+) I visited Paris last year.
(-) I didn't visit Paris last year.
(?) Did you visit Paris?

(+) She went to school.
(-) She didn't go to school.
(?) Did she go to school?
```

**Signal words:** yesterday, last (week/year), ago, in 1990, then, when, once

---

### 6. Past Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + was/were + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Action in progress at a specific past time: _At 9pm, I was watching TV._
- Interrupted action: _I was sleeping when he called._
- Two parallel actions in the past: _She was cooking while he was cleaning._
- Background in a story: _It was raining. People were running for shelter._

```
(+) I was reading when she called.
(-) I was not reading at that time.
(?) Were you reading when she called?
```

**Signal words:** while, when, at that time, at 9 o'clock (past time)

---

### 7. Past Perfect
**Formula:** `Subject + had + V3`

**Use for:**
- Action completed BEFORE another past action (further back in the past)
- The "earlier" of two past events

```
(+) I had already eaten when she arrived.
(-) I hadn't eaten before she arrived.
(?) Had you eaten before she arrived?
```

**Timeline visual:**
```
PAST PERFECT              PAST SIMPLE        NOW
      |                         |              |
[I had eaten]    --------> [she arrived] --> today
```

**Signal words:** before, after, already, by the time, when, just, never...before

---

### 8. Past Perfect Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + had + been + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Activity in progress before a specific past time
- Duration of an activity before a past event

```
(+) I had been working for 3 hours when she called.
(-) I hadn't been sleeping long when the alarm rang.
(?) How long had you been waiting?
```

## FUTURE TENSES

### 9. Future Simple (WILL)
**Formula:** `Subject + will + base verb`

**Use for:**
- Predictions (no evidence): _It will rain tomorrow._
- Instant decisions (made now): _I'll have the pizza._
- Promises: _I will call you._
- Offers/offers: _I'll help you with that._

```
(+) I will call you tonight.
(-) I will not (won't) call you.
(?) Will you call me?
```

**Signal words:** tomorrow, next week/year, in 2030, soon, probably

---

### 10. Future Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + will + be + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Action in progress at a specific future time: _At 9pm tomorrow, I will be sleeping._
- Planned/expected future activity: _She will be presenting at the conference._

```
(+) This time tomorrow, I will be flying to London.
(-) I won't be working tomorrow evening.
(?) Will you be using the car tonight?
```

---

### 11. Future Perfect
**Formula:** `Subject + will + have + V3`

**Use for:**
- Action that will be completed BEFORE a specific future time

```
(+) By the time you arrive, I will have cooked dinner.
(-) I won't have finished by then.
(?) Will you have left by 8pm?
```

**Timeline:**
```
NOW              FUTURE PERFECT          DEADLINE
 |                     |                     |
today    -->   [cooked dinner] -->   [you arrive 7pm]
```

**Signal words:** by (a time), by the time, before

---

### 12. Future Perfect Continuous
**Formula:** `Subject + will + have + been + V-ing`

**Use for:**
- Duration of an action up to a point in the future

```
(+) By 2030, I will have been studying English for 10 years.
(?) How long will you have been working here by December?
```

---

## "BE GOING TO" — Another Future Form

> Not one of the 12, but VERY commonly used!

**Use for:**
- Plans already decided: _I am going to visit my parents this weekend._
- Predictions with evidence: _Look at those clouds — it is going to rain!_

```
(+) I am going to learn Go.
(-) I am not going to be late.
(?) Are you going to apply for the job?
```

| | WILL | GOING TO |
|---|---|---|
| Instant decision | ✅ "I'll have coffee." | ❌ |
| Pre-planned | ❌ | ✅ "I'm going to visit her." |
| Prediction (no proof) | ✅ "It will be cold." | ❌ |
| Prediction (with proof) | ❌ | ✅ "It's going to rain (dark clouds)." |

## Tense Identification Drill 🔥

Identify the tense in each sentence:

1. She **has been working** here since 2020.
2. By 8pm, I **will have finished** cooking.
3. They **were playing** football when it rained.
4. **I had never seen** such a beautiful place before.
5. He **is going to** present tomorrow.
6. We **meet** every Monday.
7. I **will call** you if I'm late.
8. She **had been crying** for hours.

**Answers:**
1. Present Perfect Continuous
2. Future Perfect
3. Past Continuous (interrupted)
4. Past Perfect
5. Going to (planned future)
6. Present Simple
7. Future Simple
8. Past Perfect Continuous

## The Golden Rules Summary

```
NOW happening?          → Present Continuous     (is/are + ing)
Regular habit?          → Present Simple          (V1 / V1+s)
Experience in life?     → Present Perfect         (have/has + V3)
Still doing it now?     → Present Perf. Cont.     (have been + ing)

Done at specific time?  → Past Simple             (V2)
Was in progress?        → Past Continuous          (was/were + ing)
Earlier of two pasts?   → Past Perfect             (had + V3)

Promise/instant decide? → Future Simple (will)     (will + V1)
Pre-planned action?     → Going to                 (am/is/are going to)
At a future moment?     → Future Continuous        (will be + ing)
Before a future point?  → Future Perfect           (will have + V3)
```
