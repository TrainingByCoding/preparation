# Meeting and Call English: Professional Lines for Real-Time Problems

> Use this during Zoom/Meet/Teams calls to sound clear and professional.

---

## 1. Audio, Connectivity, and Screen Share Problems

### Background noise
- Apologies for the background noise; there is construction nearby.
- Sorry about the noise in the background.
- If the noise is distracting, I can mute when I am not speaking.

### Voice break/cut
- I lost you for a second.
- You cut out right after the budget point.
- Your audio dropped for a moment; could you repeat that?

### Echo/buzz
- Is anyone else hearing a buzzing sound?
- I am hearing some echo on my side.
- There seems to be microphone feedback.

### Screen share issues
- I will stop and restart my screen share.
- Let me reshare; that usually fixes it.
- Can you confirm if my screen is visible now?

### Waiting for participants
- Let us give everyone one more minute to join.
- We can start in a minute once everyone hops on.
- While we wait, I will quickly recap the agenda.

---

## 2. Polite Turn-Taking Language

- May I jump in for a second?
- Sorry to interrupt; can I add one point?
- Could I finish this thought quickly?
- Please go ahead.
- Thanks, over to you.

---

## 3. Clarifying and Confirming

- Just to confirm, are we aligned on the deadline?
- If I understood correctly, we are prioritizing option B.
- Let me repeat that to make sure I got it right.
- Could you clarify the scope?

---

## 4. Buying Time Without Sounding Lost

- Great question; let me think for a moment.
- I would like to check one detail before answering.
- Give me a second to pull up the numbers.

---

## 5. Meeting Control Phrases

### Start
- Let us get started.
- I will quickly walk through the agenda.

### Move topic
- Let us move to the next item.
- Coming back to the timeline...

### Park topic
- That is important; let us park it and revisit at the end.

### End
- Before we close, let us summarize action items.
- Thanks everyone, we are done for today.

---

## 6. Action-Item Language

- I will take this offline and share an update by EOD.
- Could you own this task for now?
- Let us set a deadline for Friday.
- I will send meeting notes right after this call.

---

## 7. Common Mistakes and Better Alternatives

| Avoid | Better |
|---|---|
| Your voice is breaking. | Your audio is cutting out. |
| I will share my screen once again only. | I will reshare my screen now. |
| Please do one thing. | Could you please do this? |
| Doubt | Question / Clarification |
| Revert me | Get back to me / Let me know |

---

## 8. 60-Second Practice Script

- Hi everyone, thanks for joining.
- Apologies for the background noise; there is some construction nearby.
- Can you confirm if my screen is visible?
- Great. I will walk through three updates.
- Sorry, I lost you for a second. Could you repeat the last point?
- Let us park that discussion and return to it at the end.
- Before we close, action items are: Rahul owns pricing, I will send notes, and we meet again on Monday.
