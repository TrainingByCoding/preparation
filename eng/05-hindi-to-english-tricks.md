# Hindi → English Translation Tricks

> Hindi has patterns that don't translate word-for-word.
> Learn these rules once — speak correctly forever.

---

## The "ALL" Rule — कब-कब, कहाँ-कहाँ, कौन-कौन

> **In Hindi:** We repeat the question word (कब-कब, कहाँ-कहाँ, कौन-कौन) to show "multiple / many times / many places / many people"
> **In English:** We NEVER repeat. We add **"all"** after the question word.

### The Pattern
```
Hindi repeated word  →  English question word + ALL
कब-कब               →  When all
कहाँ-कहाँ           →  Where all
कौन-कौन             →  Who all
क्या-क्या           →  What all
किस-किस             →  Which all
```

### Examples

| Hindi | ❌ Wrong English | ✅ Correct English |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| तुम कब-कब स्कूल जाती हो? | When when do you go to school? | **When all** do you go to school? |
| तुम कहाँ-कहाँ जाओगी? | Where where will you go? | **Where all** will you go? |
| कौन-कौन वहाँ पर आया था? | Who who came there? | **Who all** came there in the event? |
| तुमने क्या-क्या खाया? | What what did you eat? | **What all** did you eat? |
| तुम किस-किस से मिले? | Which which people did you meet? | **Which all** people did you meet? |

### More Sentences to Practice
```
When all do you visit the temple?
Where all have you traveled?
Who all is coming to the party?
What all did you buy from the market?
Which all subjects do you study?
Who all knows about this?
Where all did you go on your trip?
```

> **Remember:** "all" softens and broadens the question — it asks about multiple instances, not just one.

## More Hindi → English Translation Traps

### 1. "कर रहा हूँ" vs Simple Present
```
Hindi: मैं खाना खा रहा हूँ।
❌ I eat food.       (Present Simple — wrong here)
✅ I am eating food. (Present Continuous — RIGHT)

Hindi: वो सो रहा है।
❌ He sleeps.
✅ He is sleeping.
```

### 2. "ही" — Emphasis Word
```
मैं ही करूंगा।      → I will do it myself. / I'll handle it.
वही आया था।        → He was the one who came.
अभी ही जाओ।        → Go right now.
```

### 3. "तो" — Contrast / Emphasis
```
मुझे तो नहीं पता।   → I don't know, though. / As for me, I don't know.
वो तो आएगा।        → He will come for sure. / He will definitely come.
```

### 4. "भी" — Also / Too / Even
```
मैं भी आऊंगा।       → I will come too.
वो भी नहीं जानता।   → Even he doesn't know.
यह भी गलत है।       → This is also wrong.
```

### 5. "ना" at the end — Tag Question
```
तुम आओगे, ना?      → You will come, won't you?
यह सही है, ना?     → This is correct, isn't it?
वो अच्छा इंसान है, ना? → He's a good person, isn't he?
```

### 6. "क्यों नहीं" — Why Not / Of Course
```
क्यों नहीं!         → Why not! / Of course!
मैं क्यों नहीं जाऊं? → Why shouldn't I go?
```

### 7. "चाहिए" vs "चाहता हूँ"
```
मुझे जाना चाहिए।    → I should go. / I need to go.
मैं जाना चाहता हूँ।  → I want to go.

❌ "I am wanting to go." → ✅ "I want to go."
```

### 8. Literal Translations That Are WRONG
```
❌ "I am having fever."          → ✅ I have a fever.
❌ "She is knowing the answer."  → ✅ She knows the answer.
❌ "I am understanding now."     → ✅ I understand now.
❌ "He is resembling his father."→ ✅ He resembles his father.
❌ "What is your good name?"     → ✅ What is your name? (no "good name" in English)
❌ "I did not went."             → ✅ I did not go.
❌ "I am agree with you."        → ✅ I agree with you.
❌ "She opened her shoes."       → ✅ She took off her shoes.
❌ "Wear the fan."               → ✅ Turn on the fan. / Switch on the fan.
❌ "Open the light."             → ✅ Turn on the light.
❌ "Close the light."            → ✅ Turn off the light.
```

## WH-Question Patterns — Master Table

### Making Questions Correctly
```
Formula: WH-word + auxiliary + subject + verb?
         What    +   do      +  you   + want?
```

| WH-Word | Use | Example |
|---------|-----|---------|
| **What** | thing/action/info | What do you do? What happened? |
| **Who** | person (subject) | Who called you? Who is she? |
| **Whom** | person (object, formal) | Whom did you call? |
| **Whose** | possession | Whose book is this? |
| **Which** | choice between options | Which color do you prefer? |
| **When** | time | When did you arrive? |
| **Where** | place | Where do you live? |
| **Why** | reason | Why are you late? |
| **How** | manner/way | How did you do it? |
| **How much** | uncountable | How much water do you need? |
| **How many** | countable | How many people came? |
| **How long** | duration | How long have you lived here? |
| **How often** | frequency | How often do you exercise? |
| **How far** | distance | How far is the station? |

### "All" added = Multiple Instances
| Simple | With "all" |
|--------|------------|
| Where do you go? | **Where all** do you go? |
| Who came? | **Who all** came? |
| What did you eat? | **What all** did you eat? |
| When do you study? | **When all** do you study? |

## Tag Questions — "...है ना?" Pattern

> Tag = small question attached at the end to confirm something.
> Rule: **Positive sentence → Negative tag | Negative sentence → Positive tag**

```
It's cold today, isn't it?          ← positive + negative tag
She can swim, can't she?
He works here, doesn't he?
They are coming, aren't they?
You have met him, haven't you?

You don't like it, do you?          ← negative + positive tag
She isn't coming, is she?
He hasn't called, has he?
They won't be late, will they?
```

**Tricky ones:**
```
I am late, aren't I?               ← (not "amn't I")
Let's go, shall we?
Nothing happened, did it?
Everyone loved it, didn't they?    ← everyone → they (in tag)
```

## Indirect Questions — "बताओ कहाँ है?" Pattern

> When a question is inside another sentence, word order CHANGES.

```
Direct: Where is he?
Indirect: Do you know where he is?       ← no inversion!

Direct: What time is it?
Indirect: Can you tell me what time it is?

Direct: Why did she leave?
Indirect: I wonder why she left.

Direct: Is she coming?
Indirect: I don't know if she is coming.
```

**Formula for indirect:**
```
Do you know / Can you tell me / I wonder / I want to know...
    + WH-word + subject + verb   (normal order, NOT question order)
```

**Common Mistakes:**
```
❌ Do you know where is he?         → ✅ Do you know where he is?
❌ Can you tell me what is his name? → ✅ Can you tell me what his name is?
❌ I want to know why did she go.   → ✅ I want to know why she went.
```
