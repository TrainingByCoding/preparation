# Speaking Action Verbs + While Pattern + Everyday Accident Verbs

> This module builds precision in spoken English.
> Instead of using only "talk," choose the exact action verb.

---

## 1. 10 Verbs for "Talking" (Exact Meaning)

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| chat | casual friendly talk | We chatted after class. |
| whisper | speak very quietly | She whispered the answer. |
| argue | speak angrily in disagreement | They argued about money. |
| explain | make something clear | He explained the process. |
| gossip | talk about private lives | They were gossiping at lunch. |
| announce | share official news publicly | The host announced the winner. |
| complain | express dissatisfaction | She complained about the service. |
| discuss | talk seriously to analyze | We discussed the project timeline. |
| negotiate | discuss to reach agreement | We negotiated the price. |
| persuade | convince someone | He persuaded me to apply. |

Quick contrast:
- discuss = structured, thoughtful
- argue = emotional conflict
- debate = formal structured disagreement

---

## 2. While + V-ing Pattern (करते हुए)

Core rule:
- while + verb-ing

Examples:
- Do not text while driving.
- You should not drink water while eating.
- Do not use your phone while studying.
- She listens to podcasts while cooking.

Negative advice patterns:
- You should not + base verb + while + V-ing.
- You must not + base verb + while + V-ing.

Examples:
- You should not skip safety checks while operating machinery.
- You must not cross the road while using your phone.

---

## 3. Should vs Should Not for Advice

- should = recommendation
- should not / should not = negative recommendation

Examples:
- You should wear a seat belt while driving.
- You should not eat too quickly.
- Students should not copy assignments.

Stronger alternatives:
- You are better off + V-ing
- It is advisable to + base verb

Examples:
- You are better off revising daily.
- It is advisable to keep your camera steady while recording.

## 4. Everyday Accident Verbs (Natural English)

| Situation | Natural verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| liquid falls on clothes | spill / spilled | Tea spilled on my shirt. |
| water/mud hits you suddenly | splash / splashed | I got splashed with mud. |
| hit body lightly by accident | bump / bumped | I bumped my head on the shelf. |
| hit hard with injury | bang / banged | I banged my knee on the table. |
| cut skin accidentally | cut / cut myself | I cut my finger while cooking. |
| fall due to slip | slip / slipped | I slipped on the wet floor. |
| lose balance and fall | trip / tripped | I tripped over a wire. |

Important pattern:
- get + past participle for unexpected incidents

Examples:
- I got splashed with muddy water.
- My phone got damaged in the rain.
- We got stuck in traffic.

---

## 5. Preposition Accuracy with Accident Verbs

- spill on: Coffee spilled on my notebook.
- splash with: I got splashed with paint.
- bump into: I bumped into a chair.
- collide with: The bike collided with a pole.

## 6. Mini Scenarios (Speak Practice)

Scenario A: Classroom
- We were chatting before class.
- The teacher announced a surprise test.

- I was walking home when a car drove by.
- I got splashed with muddy water.

Scenario C: Library
- Please whisper in the reading zone.
