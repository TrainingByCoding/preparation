# Common English Mistakes — Fix Your Grammar

> This file is your **error-detector**. Read it again and again.
> Every mistake here is something real people make every day.

## Category 1: Modal Verb Mistakes

### ❌ Extra "to" after modals
```
❌ I can to swim.          → ✅ I can swim.
❌ She must to go.         → ✅ She must go.
❌ You should to eat well. → ✅ You should eat well.
❌ Could you to help me?   → ✅ Could you help me?
```
> **Rule:** Modal + BASE VERB (never "to" between them)

### ❌ Adding -s to modal
```
❌ She cans cook.          → ✅ She can cook.
❌ He musts leave.         → ✅ He must leave.
❌ She wills come.         → ✅ She will come.
```
> **Rule:** Modals NEVER change. No -s, no -ing, no -ed.

### ❌ Double modal
```
❌ I might could go.       → ✅ I might be able to go.
❌ I will can do it.       → ✅ I will be able to do it.
```

### ❌ Confusing "must not" vs "don't have to"
```
❌ "You must not come early" (when you mean: it's not required)
✅ "You don't have to come early." ← not required
✅ "You must not enter." ← forbidden, not allowed
```

## Category 2: Verb Tense Mistakes

### ❌ Using Present Simple instead of Present Continuous
```
❌ I watch TV now.                → ✅ I am watching TV now.
❌ Look! She runs.                → ✅ Look! She is running.
❌ He cooks dinner at the moment. → ✅ He is cooking dinner at the moment.
```
> **Rule:** Something happening RIGHT NOW → use -ing form

### ❌ Using Present Continuous with state verbs
```
❌ I am knowing the answer.    → ✅ I know the answer.
❌ She is loving chocolate.    → ✅ She loves chocolate.
❌ I am believing you.         → ✅ I believe you.
❌ He is owning a car.         → ✅ He owns a car.
```
> **State verbs (no -ing):** know, believe, understand, love, hate, want, need, own, seem, prefer, remember, forget

### ❌ Using wrong tense with "since" vs "for"
```
❌ I am here since 2020.          → ✅ I have been here since 2020.
❌ She works here since 5 years.  → ✅ She has worked here for 5 years.
```
> **Rule:** "since" and "for" (with time duration) need **Present Perfect**

### ❌ Using "since" vs "for" wrong
```
since = a specific point in time    → since 2020, since Monday, since morning
for   = a duration of time          → for 2 years, for 3 hours, for a week

✅ I have lived here for 5 years.
✅ I have lived here since 2019.
```

### ❌ Wrong conditional tense
```
❌ If I will see him, I'll tell him.  → ✅ If I see him, I'll tell him.
❌ If I would be rich, I would help.  → ✅ If I were rich, I would help.
```

## Category 3: Article Mistakes (a / an / the)

### The Basic Rules First
```
a   → before consonant sound: a book, a car, a university (u sounds like "yoo")
an  → before vowel sound:     an apple, an hour (h is silent!), an honest man
the → specific, known, unique: the sun, the president, the book I gave you
```

### ❌ Missing article
```
❌ I want to be doctor.         → ✅ I want to be a doctor.
❌ She is engineer.             → ✅ She is an engineer.
❌ I go to school by bus.       ← ✅ (actually correct — "by bus" doesn't need article)
```

### ❌ Wrong article
```
❌ She is a best student.       → ✅ She is the best student.
❌ I saw a moon last night.     → ✅ I saw the moon last night.  (unique thing)
❌ He went to a hospital.       → depends! 
   "He is in hospital." (British — means as a patient)
   "He is in the hospital." (American)
```

### ❌ "a" vs "an" — sound rule confusion
```
❌ a honest person   → ✅ an honest person     ("h" is silent → vowel sound)
❌ an university     → ✅ a university          ("u" sounds like "yoo" → consonant sound)
❌ an European       → ✅ a European            ("E" sounds like "yoo" → consonant sound)
❌ a hour            → ✅ an hour               (h is silent)
❌ an MBA            → depends on pronunciation: "an MBA" is correct (M = "em")
```

### When NOT to use "the"
```
❌ The life is beautiful.       → ✅ Life is beautiful.       (abstract concept)
❌ I love the music.            → ✅ I love music.            (in general)
❌ The English is hard.         → ✅ English is hard.         (languages — no article)
❌ I go to the school every day.→ ✅ I go to school every day. (institution function)
```

## Category 4: Preposition Mistakes

### AT / IN / ON — Time
```
AT  = specific time / points    → at 9 o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend
IN  = months, years, periods   → in January, in 2020, in the morning, in summer
ON  = days and dates           → on Monday, on July 4th, on my birthday
```

```
❌ I'll meet you on 9 o'clock.   → ✅ I'll meet you at 9 o'clock.
❌ She was born in Monday.       → ✅ She was born on Monday.
❌ He came at July.              → ✅ He came in July.
```

### AT / IN / ON — Place
```
AT  = a point/location          → at the bus stop, at the corner, at work, at home
IN  = inside / enclosed space  → in the room, in the box, in London (large area)
ON  = surface / line           → on the table, on the wall, on the street
```

```
❌ She is on the office.         → ✅ She is in the office.
❌ I am in the bus stop.         → ✅ I am at the bus stop.
❌ The book is in the table.     → ✅ The book is on the table.
```

### Common Preposition Pairs (memorize these)
```
interested IN          → I am interested in coding.
good AT                → She is good at math.
afraid OF              → He is afraid of dogs.
different FROM         → This is different from that.
depend ON              → It depends on the weather.
congratulate ON        → Congratulations on your success!
married TO             → She is married to a doctor.
bored OF/WITH          → I am bored of this movie.
proud OF               → I am proud of you.
responsible FOR        → He is responsible for the error.
similar TO             → This is similar to that.
surprised AT/BY        → I was surprised at/by her answer.
look forward TO        → I look forward to meeting you.
```

## Category 5: Subject-Verb Agreement

```
The verb must match the subject, NOT the words in between.

❌ The group of students are ready.  → ✅ The group of students is ready.
❌ Everyone have finished.           → ✅ Everyone has finished.
❌ Neither of them are coming.       → ✅ Neither of them is coming.
❌ Each of us have a book.           → ✅ Each of us has a book.
```

**Tricky ones:**
```
everyone / someone / anyone / no one → singular
The news is...              (not "are")
Mathematics is...           (not "are")
The United States is...     (not "are") ← treats as one entity
```

## Category 6: Common Word Confusions

### Do vs Make
```
DO  → activities, tasks, work, studies
MAKE → creating, producing, food/drink

do homework / do exercise / do a favor / do business / do a course
make a mistake / make a decision / make a plan / make tea / make money / make friends

❌ I made my homework.     → ✅ I did my homework.
❌ She did a mistake.      → ✅ She made a mistake.
```

### Say vs Tell
```
say  → say something (to someone)   → She said hello. / He said that he was tired.
tell → tell someone something       → She told me her name. / Tell me the truth.

❌ She told that she was tired.      → ✅ She said that she was tired.
❌ He said me to come.               → ✅ He told me to come.
```

### Look / See / Watch
```
look  → directing your eyes (intentional effort)  → Look at that bird!
see   → perceiving with your eyes (natural)       → I can see the mountains.
watch → looking at something moving/changing      → I watch movies. / Watch this!

❌ I am seeing the TV.     → ✅ I am watching the TV.
❌ Look, I can watch you!  → ✅ Look, I can see you!
```

### Hear vs Listen
```
hear  → natural, you just receive sound    → I can hear music.
listen → active, you pay attention         → Listen to this song!

❌ I am hearing the lecture.  → ✅ I am listening to the lecture.
```

### Bring vs Take
```
bring → movement toward speaker/here       → Bring me a glass of water.
take  → movement away from here           → Take this to the kitchen.
```

### Lend vs Borrow
```
lend  → you give something to someone     → Can you lend me your pen?
borrow → you take something from someone  → Can I borrow your pen?

❌ Can you borrow me a pen?   → ✅ Can you lend me a pen?
❌ I want to lend your book.  → ✅ I want to borrow your book.
```

## Category 7: Sentence Structure Mistakes

### ❌ Double negative
```
❌ I don't know nothing.       → ✅ I don't know anything.
❌ She never says nothing.     → ✅ She never says anything.
```

### ❌ Wrong word order with adverbs
```
❌ I go always to the gym.     → ✅ I always go to the gym.
❌ She is never late? → ✅ Is she never late? (in questions, be before subject)
```
> Frequency adverbs (always, usually, often, sometimes, never) go BEFORE the main verb but AFTER "be"
```
I always eat breakfast.        (before main verb)
She is always happy.           (after "be")
```

### ❌ Wrong question formation
```
❌ Where you are going?         → ✅ Where are you going?
❌ What he is doing?            → ✅ What is he doing?
❌ Do you know where is he?     → ✅ Do you know where he is?   ← indirect question (no inversion)
```

## Quick Mistake-Spotter Exercise 🔥

Find and fix the mistakes:

1. "I am knowing English well."
2. "She can to drive very fast."
3. "He said me to come early."
4. "I have lived here since 5 years."
5. "If I will have time, I'll help."
6. "She did a big mistake."
7. "I don't know nothing about it."
8. "She is good in math."
9. "We should go in home."
10. "I was born in Monday."

**Answers:**
1. I know English well. *(state verb)*
2. She can drive very fast. *(no "to" after modal)*
3. He told me to come early. *(tell + person)*
4. I have lived here for 5 years. *(duration = "for")*
5. If I have time, I'll help. *(no "will" in if-clause)*
6. She made a big mistake. *(make a mistake)*
7. I don't know anything about it. *(no double negative)*
8. She is good at math. *(good AT)*
9. We should go home. *(no preposition with "go home")*
10. I was born on Monday. *(day = "on")*

## The Cheat Sheet for Speaking Fluently

```
→ Don't say "I am agree" → say "I agree"
→ Don't say "I am fine, thank. And you?" → say "I'm fine, thanks. How about you?"
→ Don't say "According to me" → say "In my opinion" / "I think"
→ Don't say "I am here since 2 hours" → say "I have been here for 2 hours"
→ Don't say "Tell me where is the station?" → say "Tell me where the station is."
→ Don't say "I am boring" → say "I am bored" (boring = something else is boring)
→ Don't say "I am exciting" → say "I am excited"
→ Don't say "I am interesting" → say "I am interested"
```

## Bored / Boring — The -ed vs -ing Adjective Trap

| -ING adjective | -ED adjective |
|----------------|---------------|
| boring | bored |
| interesting | interested |
| exciting | excited |
| surprising | surprised |
| confusing | confused |
| tiring | tired |
| frightening | frightened |

> **-ING** → describes the thing causing the feeling ("That movie is boring.")
> **-ED** → describes how you feel ("I am bored.")

```
✅ The lecture was interesting.      ← the lecture causes interest
✅ I was very interested in it.      ← how I felt
✅ This work is tiring.              ← the work causes tiredness
✅ I am very tired.                  ← how I feel
```
