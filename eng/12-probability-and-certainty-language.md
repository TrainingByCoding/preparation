# Probability and Certainty Language (0% -> 100%)

> Fluent speakers do not only say true/false.
> They signal confidence level precisely.

---

## 1. Confidence Ladder

| Certainty | Common word/phrase | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | definitely / certainly | She will definitely call. |
| 90% | almost certainly / very likely | They are very likely to win. |
| 80% | probably | He will probably be late. |
| 70% | likely | It is likely to rain tonight. |
| 60% | possibly / there is a good chance | We could possibly finish today. |
| 50% | maybe / perhaps | Maybe we should wait. |
| 40% | unlikely | It is unlikely to happen. |
| 30% | probably not | He will probably not join. |
| 20% | doubtful | It is doubtful that they agree. |
| 10% | hardly ever (frequency) / highly unlikely | She hardly ever misses class. |
| 0% | impossible / no chance | There is no chance of delay. |

Note:
- Use hardly ever for frequency, not for one-time probability.

---

## 2. Verb Patterns for Probability

### A) It + be + adjective + that
- It is likely that he will resign.
- It is unlikely that we will finish early.

### B) Subject + modal + base verb
- She might come.
- He could be right.
- They may postpone the launch.

### C) There is a chance + clause
- There is a chance we will get approval.
- There is a slim chance they accept it.

### D) Adverbs with main verb
- He probably forgot.
- She definitely knows.
- They certainly improved.

---

## 3. Position of Adverbs (Critical)

Correct positions:
- Subject + adverb + main verb
  - She probably knows.
- Subject + be + adverb
  - She is probably right.
- Auxiliary + adverb + main verb
  - She has definitely improved.

Common errors:
- Wrong: She knows probably.
- Right: She probably knows.

---

## 4. Spoken Alternatives by Strength

| Strong | Medium | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| I am certain. | I think so. | I am not sure. |
| No doubt about it. | Most likely. | Maybe. |
| That is guaranteed. | There is a fair chance. | It is doubtful. |

---

## 5. Agreement with Certainty

- I completely agree. (100%)
- I mostly agree. (70-80%)
- I partly agree. (50-60%)
- I am not convinced. (30-40%)
- I strongly disagree. (0-10%)

---

## 6. Smart Hedging (Professional English)

Hedging helps you sound careful and credible.

Useful hedges:
- It seems that...
- It appears that...
- Based on current data...
- At this stage...
- As far as we know...

Examples:
- Based on current data, revenue will likely grow next quarter.
- At this stage, it appears unlikely that costs will drop.

---

## 7. Mini-Dialogues

A: Will the client approve this today?
B: Probably, but not guaranteed.

A: Is this bug easy to fix?
B: Highly likely, but I need one hour to confirm.

A: Is the launch at risk?
B: There is a small chance of delay due to testing.

## 8. Practice: Choose the Right Strength

Pick the best phrase:

1. I am 100% sure. -> ___
2. I think this may happen, around 50%. -> ___
3. Very little chance. -> ___
4. I am sure it will not happen. -> ___

Sample answers:
1. definitely / certainly
2. maybe / perhaps
3. unlikely / doubtful
4. no chance / impossible
